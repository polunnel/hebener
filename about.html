About HEBENER

Elevating Industry Standards in the UAE

Our Vision

To set the benchmark for heavy lifting engineering across the GCC, recognized as the most trusted technical partner for mission-critical industrial crane infrastructure.

Our Mission

To engineer and deliver high-performance EOT cranes and material handling solutions that maximize operational safety, precision, and uptime for the Middle East’s manufacturing and construction sectors.

Leadership & Expertise

Hari Krishnan, Founder and Director, brings over 20 years of specialized experience to the UAE's material handling and industrial lifting space. As a qualified Mechanical Engineer with a Post Graduate Diploma in Marketing Management, Hari has spent decades solving complex lifting challenges and directing sales operations for the world's leading international crane manufacturers, including extensive leadership roles with Demag Cranes & Components.

This deep technical foundation is supported by a trusted network of seasoned industry experts, structural engineers, and logistics partners. By bridging the gap between world-class European engineering and localized UAE market demands, HEBENER Lifting Solutions guarantees precision, reliability, and absolute compliance for every heavy lifting requirement.
